# Evidence-bound rewrite audit

## Contribution-and-scope contract

- **Core contribution:** The paper identifies where a measured
  machine--human realization signature survives the sequence from surface
  form to hidden readout and fixed-token transport, and where the evidence
  stops at free-generation control.
- **Contribution hierarchy:** The primary result is the lexical
  surface-to-hidden trace and its behavioral boundary. The two-interface
  surface construct and source-consistent Jacobian pullback support that
  result. The bounded empirical contribution covers English continuation
  pairs, four generators, and two target-model families. The portable payoff
  is an evaluation sequence for separating readability, transport, and
  realization.
- **Claim ceiling:** Lexical and syntactic directions recur across the two
  500-pair protocols and transfer to held-out generators. Both are readable
  in Llama 3.1 8B and Qwen3 4B. Only the lexical five-layer curve exceeds all
  100 complete matched-null pipelines. Neither direction establishes stable,
  component-selective generation control under the frozen intervention and
  decoding protocol.
- **Load-bearing cautions:** English-only automatic measurements; four named
  generator environments; two target-model families; proposition-plan
  asymmetry versus prefix-only content drift; shallow discourse measures;
  five sampled checkpoints; 32 Jacobian-fit pairs; one intervention layer,
  norm, and decoding regime; limited seeds; no reader-perception study; no
  calibrated detector or deployment population.
- **Edit authority:** Whole-manuscript prose, section order, abstract,
  captions, and appendix explanation were authorized. Equations, frozen
  numerical results, citations, labels, table cells, and the identity of
  experimental conditions were treated as locked.
- **Live queries:** None. The rewrite does not change research questions,
  data admission, or the status of a reported result.

## Section-level concentration map

- **Abstract and Introduction:** contribution and break point now appear
  before procedural qualification.
- **Related Work:** comparison is limited to the distinction needed to define
  the target and evaluation burden.
- **Data and Method:** real confounds remain adjacent to the construction they
  qualify; repeated assurances were removed.
- **Results:** each paragraph leads with an estimate-backed finding. Negative
  results are reported as results rather than failed objectives.
- **Discussion and Conclusion:** interpretation follows the three estimands;
  detailed cautions are concentrated in Limitations and Ethics.
- **Appendix:** prose explains evidence and scope rather than document-design
  history.

## Claim/evidence regression

- The title, abstract, introduction, results, discussion, and conclusion share
  the same hierarchy: recurrent surface direction; lexical hidden trace;
  unresolved selective realization.
- No causal verb was strengthened beyond the fixed-token intervention. Free
  generation remains an effect audit, not evidence of stable control.
- Proposition-conditioned and prefix-only evidence retain distinct source
  status and opposing validity trade-offs.
- The discourse direction remains an off-target audit; it was not promoted to
  a confirmatory target.
- The one-seed Qwen inversion remains a sealed observation whose independent
  replication is sign-unstable.
- All citations, equations, cross-references, numerical estimates, model
  identities, sample sizes, and table values retain their evidentiary roles.
- References begin on page 8; the seven-page main paper stays within the
  eight-page ACL limit without a partially empty eighth page.

## Deliberate non-edits

- The paper does not claim a universal machine-authorship factor, a validated
  human perception of “AI-like” prose, or a deployable detector.
- The syntactic result remains non-exceptional under the matched null despite
  high sign agreement.
- Llama syntax remains positive on target projection but unresolved on
  component selectivity.
- Intervals containing zero are described as unresolved, never as proof of an
  exact null.
- The frozen equations, table cells, and experimental settings were not
  stylistically simplified when doing so could alter interpretation.
